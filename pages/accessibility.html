    Accessibility

    Store Access

    Tek Media Group welcomes customers with disabilities. Handicap and wheelchair access to our store is at the back of the building.

    Tek Media Group
    711 South Victory Blvd
    Burbank, CA 91502

    Appointments and Directions

    Please call us at (818) 244-4440 to make an appointment or to get directions to the accessible entrance at the back of the store. We are happy to help you plan your visit.

    • Monday–Friday: 8:30 AM – 5:00 PM
    • Weekends, holidays and after hours: by appointment only

    If a visit is difficult, ask us about pickup and delivery of your equipment.

    Website Accessibility Policy

    Tek Media Group is committed to making this website usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification or other assistive technology. We work toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA and review our pages as we update them.

    Each page has a Listen to this page link in the footer that reads the page content aloud.

    Tell Us About a Problem

    If you cannot use part of our store or website, or you need information in another format, contact us and we will work with you to fix it or provide the information another way.

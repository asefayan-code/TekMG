About the McIntosh MC4275 & Our Service

The McIntosh MC4275 is a classic solid-state stereo amplifier from the mid-1970s, delivering 75 watts per channel with McIntosh's legendary Autoformer output transformers. Produced during McIntosh's transition to solid-state, the MC4275 combines the reliability of silicon transistors with the signature McIntosh sound enabled by the Autoformer outputs. With its classic glass front panel, brushed aluminum chassis, and unmistakable McIntosh styling, the MC4275 is highly sought after by collectors and audiophiles who want vintage McIntosh power and reliability.

The McIntosh MC4275 – Solid-State Powerhouse

🎵 McINTOSH MC4275 KEY FEATURES:
  • Power Output: 75 watts per channel (solid-state)
  • Autoformer Outputs: McIntosh's legendary output transformers for stable 4/8/16 ohm operation
  • Complementary Symmetry: All-silicon transistor design for low distortion
  • Glass Front Panel: Classic McIntosh aesthetic with backlit glass
  • Power Guard: McIntosh's proprietary distortion-limiting circuit (on later models)
  • Sentinel Monitoring: Power output monitoring system
  • Rack Mountable: Professional-style chassis with rack mount options
⚠️ IMPORTANT – MC4275 SPECIFIC CONSIDERATIONS: The MC4275 uses electrolytic capacitors that are known to fail with age. Failed capacitors can cause noise, hum, and can damage output transistors. The Autoformers are robust but should be tested for proper operation. We have the specific expertise and test equipment to service this amplifier correctly.

Common McIntosh MC4275 Problems we Solve

#1 Electrolytic Capacitor Failure

Symptom: Excessive hum, low output power, unstable operation, noise.
Our Solution: We replace all electrolytic capacitors in the power supply and signal paths.

#2 Output Transistor Failure

Symptom: Distortion, one channel dead, blown fuse, DC at outputs.
Our Solution: We test all output transistors, replace failed units with matched modern equivalents.

#3 Tantalum Capacitor Failure

Symptom: Short circuits, noise, distortion, intermittent operation.
Our Solution: We replace all tantalum capacitors with high-quality modern electrolytics or film caps.

#4 Autoformer Issues

Symptom: Low output, distorted sound, one channel weak.
Our Solution: We test Autoformer for opens or shorts, verify proper impedance matching.

#5 Drifted Resistors

Symptom: Off-spec voltages, poor performance, bias instability.
Our Solution: We test all carbon comp resistors and replace any drifted more than 10% from nominal.

#6 Noisy Transistors

Symptom: Hiss, crackling, intermittent noise, especially in input stage.
Our Solution: We test all transistors and replace noisy units with modern equivalents.

#7 Speaker Relay Not Engaging

Symptom: No sound, relay clicks but no output, intermittent output.
Our Solution: We test relay contacts, replace if oxidized, verify protection circuit timing.

#8 Power Guard Not Working

Symptom: No Power Guard indication, distortion at high levels.
Our Solution: We test and calibrate Power Guard circuit to factory specifications.

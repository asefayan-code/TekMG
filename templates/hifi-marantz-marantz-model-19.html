About the Marantz Model 19 & Our Approach

The Marantz Model 19 (1969-1971) is the successor to the groundbreaking Model 18, featuring significant improvements including a 5-gang FM tuner for better selectivity and sensitivity, an upgraded amplifier section with 50 watts per channel, and the same legendary build quality. It's highly sought after by collectors for its exceptional sound quality, superior tuner performance, and classic Marantz aesthetics. We've restored dozens of Model 19 receivers over three decades, and we treat each one with the meticulous care this American classic deserves. We use premium replacement components and follow factory service procedures to ensure your receiver performs to original specifications.

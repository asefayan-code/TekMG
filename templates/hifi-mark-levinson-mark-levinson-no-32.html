About the Mark Levinson No. 32 Reference & Our Approach

The Mark Levinson No. 32 Reference preamplifier (circa 2000-2007) represents the absolute pinnacle of solid-state preamplifier design. Featuring a dual-chassis configuration (separate power supply and control/audio chassis), a fully discrete, balanced, dual-mono topology, and a legendary R-2R ladder volume control, the No. 32 set new standards for transparency, dynamics, and musicality. It remains highly sought after by discerning audiophiles worldwide. We've restored dozens of Mark Levinson preamplifiers over three decades, and we treat each No. 32 with meticulous care, using premium replacement parts while preserving the original reference-grade sonic character.

Deep Dive: Our Mark Levinson No. 32 Restoration Process

⚡ POWER SUPPLY CHASSIS (Critical for Reference Performance)
  • Main Filter Capacitors: Large snap-in electrolytics in the separate power supply chassis – we replace with high-ripple, low-ESR, 105°C rated types (Nichicon KG, Cornell Dubilier, or Panasonic).
  • Regulator Section: We replace all small electrolytic capacitors on the regulator boards, verify voltage rails for both analog and digital sections.
  • Bridge Rectifiers: We test and replace if noisy, upgrade to ultra-fast soft recovery types for lower noise floor.
  • AC Input & Safety: We inspect AC input filter, replace RIFA caps if present, check fuses and IEC inlet.
  • Power Supply Umbilical: We check the umbilical cable and connectors for wear, clean contacts.
🎛️ AUDIO CHASSIS – SIGNAL PATH RESTORATION
COMPLETE AUDIO CIRCUIT RESTORATION:
  • Electrolytic Capacitors: All coupling and power supply decoupling caps on the audio boards are replaced with audio-grade types (Nichicon FG, Elna Silmic II, or WIMA films for small values).
  • Gain Modules: The proprietary gain modules are carefully inspected, recapped, and calibrated. These are the heart of the No. 32's sound.
  • Volume Control (R-2R Ladder): We verify the resistor ladder accuracy, check control logic, and replace any faulty relays in the attenuator circuit.
  • DC Offset Calibration: We adjust DC offset at outputs to near 0mV for optimal performance with any power amplifier.
  • Channel Matching: We ensure both channels are matched within 0.1dB across the entire volume range.
🔊 INPUT / OUTPUT SECTION
  • Input Relays: The No. 32 uses high-quality relays for input switching. We test and replace any that show increased contact resistance.
  • Connector Cleaning: We clean all RCA and XLR connectors with DeoxIT, tighten any loose jacks.
  • Balanced Line Driver/Receiver: We verify proper operation of balanced input and output stages, check common-mode rejection.
📟 CONTROL & DISPLAY BOARD
  • Microprocessor Supply Caps: Replace electrolytic capacitors on the control board to ensure stable operation of the logic circuits.
  • Display VFD Check: We verify the vacuum fluorescent display for brightness and missing segments. Replacement displays are available if needed.
  • Front Panel Controls: We clean the volume encoder and button contacts, ensuring smooth operation.
  • Remote Control Sensor: We check IR receiver operation, replace if weak.

Common Mark Levinson No. 32 Problems we Solve

#1 Capacitor Aging in Power Supply

Symptom: Increased noise floor, loss of transparency, hum, reduced dynamics.
Our Solution: Complete recap of all electrolytic capacitors in both chassis, focusing on power supply and regulator sections.

#2 Volume Control Issues

Symptom: Skipping steps, uneven channel balance, noisy volume changes, stuck at certain levels.
Our Solution: Clean or replace relays in the R-2R ladder, check control logic, recalibrate volume mapping.

#3 DC Offset Drift

Symptom: Output relay protection triggering, audible thump on power on/off, DC at outputs.
Our Solution: Replace aging input differential pair transistors (if discrete), recalibrate DC offset, replace capacitors in servo circuit.

#4 One Channel Distorted / Low Output

Symptom: Imbalance between channels, distortion on one side.
Our Solution: Check gain modules, replace capacitors in signal path, verify relay contacts, check for failed op-amps.

#5 Display Dim or Missing

Symptom: VFD display dim, flickering, or not working.
Our Solution: Check filament voltage, replace driver capacitors, replace VFD if necessary.

#6 Remote Control Not Working

Symptom: No response to remote commands.
Our Solution: Check IR sensor on front panel, replace electrolytic caps on control board, verify remote transmitter.

#7 Input Switching Issues

Symptom: Input not switching, intermittent connection, noise when switching.
Our Solution: Replace input relays, clean contacts, check control logic.

Precision Calibration & Alignment Procedure

🎛️ OUR CALIBRATION PROCESS FOR NO. 32:
  • Power Supply Verification: We measure all voltage rails (analog ±15V, ±24V; digital +5V, +3.3V) to within ±1% tolerance.
  • DC Offset Adjustment: Using a precision DMM, we adjust DC offset at main outputs to <±1mV.
  • Channel Matching: We verify gain matching between left and right channels within ±0.05dB across the entire frequency range.
  • Volume Control Tracking: We test volume step accuracy and channel tracking from minimum to maximum, ensuring <0.5dB tracking error.
  • Gain Module Calibration: For adjustable gain modules, we set gain precisely to factory specifications.
  • Distortion Measurement: We measure THD+N at various frequencies and levels, targeting <0.001% at 1kHz.
  • Noise Floor: We verify signal-to-noise ratio >115dB A-weighted.

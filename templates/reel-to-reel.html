Questions

Which reel-to-reel brands do you repair?

Studer, Akai, Fostex, Teac, Tascam, Pioneer, Technics, Aiwa, Revox, Ampex, Dokorder and others. If your brand is not listed, start a service request and we will take a look.

Do you repair reel-to-reel recorders nationwide?

Yes. Ship your machine to our Burbank, CA shop, or ask about pickup and delivery.

My deck plays but sounds dull or noisy. What is wrong?

Common causes are dirty or worn heads, misalignment, aging capacitors or a worn pinch roller. We inspect and service these as part of an estimate.

How much does a repair cost?

Estimates are free. The cost depends on the model and what the inspection finds.

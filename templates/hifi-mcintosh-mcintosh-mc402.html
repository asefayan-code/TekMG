⚙️ MCINTOSH MC402 power amplifier · technical digest

Field-collected data and original specifications for professional diagnosis. Official service bulletins are not public; the following is compiled from technical discussions and manufacturer documentation.

Common complaint & field‑issue analysis

🔹 Ground loop hum Documented interaction with preamplifiers (e.g. C46). A McIntosh engineer emphasised: verify all components on same electrical phase before any ground isolation — safety ground must remain intact.
🔸 EPJ94VO board failure One specific failure mode traced to EPJ94VO (Rohs 129711 - Rev. A). Symptom: audio drops / diminishes after ≈1 hour, first one channel then the other → points to thermal or failing component on that board.

Capacitor selection · original specifications

🔬 original design: precision metal film resistors and low dielectric absorption film capacitors in all critical signal paths.
📋 replacement strategy: McIntosh explicitly requires parts either specified by McIntosh or with identical characteristics as original. Unauthorised substitutions can pose safety hazards.

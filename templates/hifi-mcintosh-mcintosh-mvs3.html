Common MVS3 Problems & Failures
Power Supply Issues
  • Failed power supply capacitors causing voltage instability
  • Overheated voltage regulators and diodes
  • Transformer hum or overheating
  • Intermittent power cycling
  • Display backlight failure
Audio Processing Failures
  • Loss of surround channels (center, rear, subwoofer)
  • Digital audio decoding failures
  • Crackling or popping sounds from aged capacitors
  • Volume control irregularities
  • Intermittent audio dropouts
Video & Control Problems
  • Failed video switching circuitry
  • Remote control system failures
  • Microprocessor lockups or resets
  • Display driver IC failures
  • Input/output selection problems

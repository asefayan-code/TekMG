Complete reference guide for McIntosh MXA60 error codes with repair solutions and capacitor recommendations
Error Code Description Repair Solution Common Capacitors to Check/Replace
E01 / PROTECT Speaker protection triggered (DC offset, overcurrent) Check output transistors for shorts. Test DC offset at speaker terminals (should be <50mV). Inspect driver transistors and small signal transistors on amp board. Power supply filter caps, small electrolytics on protection circuit board.
E02 / TEMP Amplifier overheating Clean dust from heatsinks and internal fans. Ensure fans are operational. Check for failing output transistors causing excess heat. Not primarily a cap issue, but check caps on fan power supply board if present.
No Display / No Power Unit completely dead Check main AC fuse. Test standby power supply for +/- 5V or 3.3V. Inspect for bulging/leaking primary power supply capacitors. Main filter caps (high value, high voltage), smaller electrolytics on standby power supply.
Intermittent Operation Audio cuts in/out, random faults Reseat all internal ribbon cables and connectors. Check for cold solder joints, especially on large power supply pins and transformer connections. All electrolytic capacitors on main power supply and amplifier modules. ESR meter testing recommended.

General Capacitor Replacement Notes

Important: Common failure points are the main power supply filter capacitors and smaller electrolytics on the various regulation and control boards. Always use high-quality, low-ESR replacements with the same or higher voltage/temperature ratings.

Best Practices for McIntosh MXA60 Repair

  • Safety First: Always disconnect power and discharge capacitors before servicing
  • Use Quality Parts: Replace capacitors with high-quality, low-ESR components
  • Match Specifications: Ensure voltage and temperature ratings meet or exceed original specs
  • ESR Testing: Use an ESR meter to identify failing capacitors before they cause damage
  • Professional Service: Complex repairs should be performed by qualified technicians

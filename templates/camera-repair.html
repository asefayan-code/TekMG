Common Problems We Fix

If your camera shows any of these symptoms, we can diagnose and repair it.

  • No power
  • Soft back focus
  • Battery does not charge
  • SDI port not working
  • SDI port yanked out or damaged
  • Dropped / physical damage
  • Salt, sand or dust inside the camera
  • LCD display not working
  • Iris blades stuck
  • No video or audio
  • SD card stuck or inserted backwards
  • Liquid damage

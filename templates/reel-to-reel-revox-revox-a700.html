Overview

⚠️ CRITICAL SAFETY WARNING: The Revox A700 contains multiple RIFA paper capacitors that are known to explode, smoke, and cause collateral damage.

Common Failure Modes

#1 RIFA Paper Capacitors (EMI Suppression)

Symptom: Smoke, burning smell, cracking sounds, blown fuses, erratic operation.

Cause: Paper dielectric absorbs moisture, cracks, becomes resistive, and eventually short-circuits.

Repair: Replace ALL RIFA capacitors on sight before power-up. Typically 7-9 units across the relay board, motor boards, and mains input.

#2 FRAKO Electrolytic Capacitors

Symptom: Power supply ripple, motor control instability, audio distortion, logic glitches.

Cause: Gold FRAKO electrolytics dry out and lose capacitance (often measuring 1/3 of nominal value).

Repair: Replace all FRAKO electrolytics in power supplies and audio paths.

#3 Red Devil Tantalum Capacitors

Symptom: Short circuits, destroyed ICs (TBA931, TCA561), erratic logic behavior.

Repair: Replace all tantalum capacitors on audio and logic boards. Use higher voltage ratings (35V instead of 16V) or film substitutes.

#4 Motor Run Capacitors

Symptom: Motors run hot, won't start, slow speed, capstan instability.

Cause: 3.5µF (capstan) and 6.5+1.5µF (reel motor) capacitors degrade.

Repair: Replace with new AC-rated motor run capacitors.

#5 TCA561 / BC108 Transistor Failures

Symptom: Reel motor issues, capstan loses lock, intermittent operation that worsens with heat.

Cause: BC108 transistors (Vceo=20V) are under-specified for 24V rail; failures destroy TCA561 ICs.

Repair: Replace BC108 with higher voltage transistors (BC337, BC547). Consider TCA561 replacement modules.

#6 Slide Pots (Faders)

Symptom: Noisy/crackling controls, intermittent signal, dead channels.

Cause: Mylar dust shields trap contaminants; original lubricants dry out.

Repair: Remove mylar strips, clean with DeOxit, exercise controls.

#7 Transport Mode Switch Contamination

Symptom: Machine enters wrong mode at power-on (e.g., fast wind without command).

Cause: Tracking between gold contacts on transport PCB.

Repair: Split switch, clean contacts with IPA.

#8 Capstan Motor Lubrication

Symptom: Speed lamps delayed or fail to light, capstan servo won't lock.

Cause: Dried lubricant in felt rings.

Repair: Drop shaft, apply PDP65 oil to felt rings (2 top, 1 bottom).

Systematic Restoration Process

🔧 PRO TIP: Work methodically in stages. Remove the audio section as a block to access transport. Test after each stage. Solder pads lift easily—use care and proper temperature.

Recommended Restoration Order:

  1. Stage 1 - RIFA Removal: Replace all RIFA capacitors on motor boards, relay board, and mains input BEFORE applying power.
  2. Stage 2 - Power Supply: Replace FRAKO electrolytics, check rectifiers, test voltages.
  3. Stage 3 - Transport Test: Verify basic transport functions.
  4. Stage 4 - Capstan Board: Replace capacitors, check TCA561, update BC108 transistors.
  5. Stage 5 - Logic Board: Replace tantalums, upgrade vulnerable transistors.
  6. Stage 6 - Mechanical: Clean/lube capstan motor, replace reel motor bearings, service tension arms.
  7. Stage 7 - Audio Section: Replace all electrolytics and tantalums, clean slide pots, replace trimmers.
  8. Stage 8 - Calibration: Full electronic alignment.

Critical Transistor Upgrades

The A700 uses several under-specified transistors that should be upgraded during restoration to prevent IC damage.

Location Original Problem Recommended Replacement
Q17 (Tape Control Board) BC108B (20V) Vceo too low for 24V rail; failures destroy TCA561 BC337 (45V) or BC547 (45V)
Q5 (Capstan Board) BC108 (20V) Same voltage vulnerability BC337 or BC547
Q1 (Optical shut-off) BC109C (20V) Under-designed for 24V BC107/BC547 (45V)
General purpose BC107/108/109 Mixed voltage ratings BC547 (modern equivalent)

Vulnerable ICs & Protection

Critical ICs to Protect:

  • TBA931 - Audio IC (can be destroyed by shorted tantalums)
  • SC10429 - Logic IC (unobtainable)
  • TDA1000 - Logic/control
💡 Protection Strategy: Replace all tantalum capacitors and upgrade vulnerable transistors BEFORE powering up to avoid destroying these ICs.

For TCA561 failures, consider Mister Q's plug-in replacement solution (ESP32-based) rather than hunting for NOS chips.

Mechanical Maintenance

Slide Pot Cleaning

  1. Remove front panel
  2. Gently pry out mylar film strips from each fader using jeweler's screwdriver
  3. Spray DeOxit into exposed fader opening
  4. Work control up/down to distribute cleaner

Capstan Motor Lubrication

  • Use genuine PDP65 oil
  • Drop shaft, apply to felt rings (2 top, 1 bottom)
  • Essential for proper servo lock and speed lamp operation

Reel Motor Bearings

  • Replace bearings for quiet operation
  • Clean and re-lubricate

Transport Mode Switch

  • If machine enters mode at power-on, split switch and clean gold contacts with IPA

Tension Arms

  • If damping lost, disassemble, clean, replace silicon damping fluid

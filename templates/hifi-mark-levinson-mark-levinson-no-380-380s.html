About the Mark Levinson No. 380 / 380S & Our Approach

The Mark Levinson No. 380 (introduced 1996) and No. 380S (introduced 1999) are reference-grade preamplifiers that defined high-end audio performance for their era. Featuring a fully balanced, dual-mono topology, precision stepped volume control, and a sophisticated power supply, the 380 series delivers stunning transparency, dynamics, and musicality. The 380S added improved components, upgraded power supply, and refined circuitry. Both models remain highly sought after by discerning audiophiles worldwide. Deep Dive: Our Mark Levinson No. 380/380S Restoration Process

⚡ POWER SUPPLY (Critical for Low Noise)
  • Main Filter Capacitors: Large snap-in electrolytics in the power supply – we replace with high-ripple, low-ESR, 105°C rated types (Nichicon KG, Panasonic, or Cornell Dubilier).
  • Regulator Section: We replace all small electrolytic capacitors on the regulator boards, verify voltage rails (±15V, +5V, +5VA, etc.) within tolerance.
  • Bridge Rectifiers: We test and replace if noisy, upgrade to ultra-fast soft recovery types for lower noise floor.
  • AC Input & Safety: We inspect AC input filter, replace RIFA caps if present, check fuses and IEC inlet.
  • Transformer Check: We verify toroidal transformer output voltages and listen for mechanical hum.
🎛️ AUDIO BOARD RESTORATION (Dual-Mono Design)
COMPLETE SIGNAL PATH RESTORATION:
  • Electrolytic Capacitors: All coupling and power supply decoupling caps on the audio boards are replaced with audio-grade types (Nichicon FG, Elna Silmic II, or WIMA films for small values).
  • Discrete Stage Service: The No. 380 uses discrete transistor gain stages. We test transistors and replace any that are noisy or out of spec.
  • Op-Amp Upgrade (Optional): For units with op-amps, we can upgrade to modern low-noise types (OPA1611, OPA1656) if desired.
  • Feedback Network: We verify resistor values and replace any that have drifted.
🔊 VOLUME CONTROL (Stepped Attenuator)
  • Relay Testing: The No. 380 uses a relay-switched stepped attenuator for precision volume control. We test each relay for contact resistance and proper operation.
  • Relay Replacement: We replace any relays that are intermittent, noisy, or have high contact resistance.
  • Resistor Ladder Check: We verify the precision resistor ladder values, replace any drifted resistors.
  • Channel Tracking: We verify volume tracking between left and right channels within 0.5dB across the entire range.
🔄 INPUT SWITCHING & CONNECTORS
  • Input Relays: The No. 380 uses high-quality relays for input switching. We test and replace any that show increased contact resistance or intermittent operation.
  • Connector Cleaning: We clean all RCA and XLR connectors with DeoxIT, tighten any loose jacks.
  • Balanced Line Driver/Receiver: We verify proper operation of balanced input and output stages.
📟 CONTROL & DISPLAY BOARD
  • Microprocessor Supply Caps: Replace electrolytic capacitors on the control board to ensure stable operation of the logic circuits.
  • Display VFD Check: We verify the vacuum fluorescent display for brightness and missing segments. Replacement displays are available if needed.
  • Front Panel Controls: We clean the volume encoder and button contacts, ensuring smooth operation.
  • Remote Control Sensor: We check IR receiver operation, replace if weak.

Common Mark Levinson No. 380/380S Problems we Solve

#1 Capacitor Aging in Power Supply

Symptom: Increased noise floor, loss of transparency, hum, reduced dynamics.
Our Solution: Complete recap of all electrolytic capacitors in the power supply and regulator sections.

#2 Volume Control Issues (Relay Noise / Skipping)

Symptom: Clicking or popping when changing volume, uneven channel balance, steps not tracking correctly.
Our Solution: Test and replace faulty relays in the stepped attenuator, verify resistor ladder, recalibrate volume mapping.

#3 DC Offset Drift

Symptom: Output relay protection triggering, audible thump on power on/off, DC at outputs.
Our Solution: Replace aging input differential pair transistors, recalibrate DC offset to near 0mV.

#4 One Channel Distorted / Low Output

Symptom: Imbalance between channels, distortion on one side.
Our Solution: Check audio board components, replace capacitors, test discrete transistors, verify relay contacts.

#5 Input Switching Issues

Symptom: Input not switching, intermittent connection, noise when switching, no sound on certain inputs.
Our Solution: Replace input relays, clean contacts, check control logic.

#6 Display Dim or Missing

Symptom: VFD display dim, flickering, or not working.
Our Solution: Check filament voltage, replace driver capacitors, replace VFD if necessary.

#7 Remote Control Not Working

Symptom: No response to remote commands.
Our Solution: Check IR sensor on front panel, replace electrolytic caps on control board, verify remote transmitter.

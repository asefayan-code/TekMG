Troubleshooting

Symptom & solution.

SymptomRepair solution
Speaker completely deadReplace the blown internal fuse; repair or replace the power supply board
Distorted or muffled soundReplace deteriorated foam surrounds; repair or replace damaged drivers
Loud hum or buzzReplace failing electrolytic capacitors; check ground connections
Intermittent cutting outRepair the thermal protection circuit; resolder loose PCB connections
Red LED or error indicatorDiagnose and repair the amplifier protection circuit; replace the damaged output stage
No bass responseReplace the blown woofer; repair the bass amplifier module; check the crossover
Crackling when playingRecone drivers; clean the voice coil; replace damaged dust caps
Won't connect wirelesslyReplace the Wi-Fi or Bluetooth module; repair antenna connections
Penta cone damageFull cone replacement and driver refurbishment
Acoustic lens issues (BeoLab 5)Repair or replace the acoustic lens assembly; realign the tweeter

Handle with care

  • All BeoLab speakers contain active amplification. Never open one without proper ESD protection.
  • Many models use proprietary ICEpower amplifier modules that need specialized replacement.
  • Foam surrounds deteriorate over time, especially on models 20+ years old, and should be replaced proactively.
  • Wireless models need firmware updates and calibration after a board replacement.
  • Original B&O drivers are unavailable for many vintage models, so reconing is required.

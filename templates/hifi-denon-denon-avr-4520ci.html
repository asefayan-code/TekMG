About the Denon AVR-4520CI & Our Service

The Denon AVR-4520CI is a legendary flagship AV receiver from Denon's prestigious CI (Custom Installation) series. Produced from 2012 to 2014, this receiver represents the pinnacle of Denon's engineering during the early 2010s. Featuring 11 discrete amplifier channels (150 watts each), a massive toroidal transformer, premium DACs, advanced video processing from Analog Devices, and Audyssey MultEQ XT32 room correction, the 4520CI was designed for the most demanding home theater systems. Weighing over 40 pounds, the 4520CI was built to deliver uncompromising performance for custom installers and enthusiasts alike.

Common Denon AVR-4520CI Problems we Solve

#1 Power Supply / Main Filter Capacitor Failure

Symptom: Excessive hum, low output power, receiver won't turn on, protection mode.
Our Solution: We replace the massive main filter capacitors in the toroidal power supply, test rectifiers, verify all voltage rails.

#2 HDMI Board Failure (Common)

Symptom: No video, intermittent signal, "no signal" message, HDCP errors.
Our Solution: We diagnose the HDMI board, replace failed switch ICs, repair EDID memory, restore full HDMI functionality.

#3 Amplifier Channel Dropout

Symptom: One or more channels dead, distorted, or cutting out.
Our Solution: We test all 11 discrete amplifier channels, replace failed output transistors, match replacements, adjust bias.

#4 Protection Mode / Won't Power On

Symptom: Receiver goes into protection mode, clicks but no sound, won't turn on.
Our Solution: We diagnose shorted outputs, check power supply caps, verify protection circuit, repair as needed.

#5 No Audio / Intermittent Audio

Symptom: No sound from speakers, sound cuts in and out on some channels.
Our Solution: We test amplifier channels, speaker relays, input switching, DSP audio path, and DACs.

#6 Speaker Relay Issues

Symptom: Relay clicks but no output, intermittent output, channel dropout.
Our Solution: We test all 11 channel relays, clean or replace oxidized contacts, verify protection circuit timing.

#7 Audyssey Calibration Issues

Symptom: Microphone not detected, calibration fails, poor results.
Our Solution: We test microphone input, verify DSP processing, perform manual calibration if needed, update firmware.

#8 Video Processing Failure

Symptom: No upscaling, poor video quality, scaling artifacts.
Our Solution: We test Analog Devices video processor, verify 4K upscaling, repair or replace failed components.

#9 Network / Streaming Issues

Symptom: No Ethernet connection, streaming not working, network settings lost.
Our Solution: We test network module, perform firmware updates, reset network settings, repair or replace failed components.

#10 Overheating / Fan Noise

Symptom: Receiver gets very hot, fan runs constantly, thermal shutdown.
Our Solution: We clean massive heat sinks, verify bias settings, check for proper ventilation, test fan operation.

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What are common problems with the AVR-4520CI?

Common issues include main filter capacitor failure in the toroidal power supply, HDMI board failures (common in this generation), amplifier channel dropout from output transistor or relay issues, and protection mode from aged components. We've seen and repaired all of these problems.

❓ Can you fix the HDMI board issues?

Yes. The HDMI board on the 4520CI is a common failure point. We have specialized diagnostic equipment for HDMI repair. We can diagnose and replace failed switch ICs, repair EDID memory issues, and restore full HDMI functionality.

❓ Can you replace the main filter capacitors?

Yes. The main filter capacitors in the toroidal power supply are a critical failure point. We replace them with high-quality 105°C Japanese capacitors (Nichicon or Panasonic) for improved reliability and performance.

❓ Do you calibrate Audyssey?

Yes. We can perform a full Audyssey MultEQ XT32 calibration using the supplied microphone, optimizing your system for your room acoustics. We also have access to Audyssey Pro calibration for advanced users.

Common MX110 Problems & Failures
Power Supply Issues
  • Failed filter capacitors causing hum and noise
  • Selenium rectifier failure and toxic fumes
  • Voltage regulator tube (0A2) aging or failure
  • Power transformer overheating
  • Fuse blowing due to capacitor shorts
Audio Circuit Failures
  • Aged coupling capacitors causing loss of bass response
  • Noisy or intermittent tone controls
  • Scratchy volume control potentiometers
  • Channel imbalance due to component drift
  • Output coupling capacitor leakage
Tuner & Switching Problems
  • FM tuner alignment drift causing poor reception
  • Oxidized relay contacts causing intermittent audio
  • Worn input selector switches
  • Tuning dial string breakage or slippage
  • Tuner lamp failures affecting dial illumination
Recommended Component Replacements
Component Type Location/Function Recommended Replacement Failure Symptoms
Electrolytic Capacitors Power Supply Filter Nichicon KG/KL series, Rubycon YXG 120Hz hum, reduced bass, distortion
Selenium Rectifier Power Supply Silicon diode bridge (1N4007) Foul odor, loss of power, overheating
Coupling Capacitors Audio Signal Path Audyn Cap, Solen, WIMA film caps Loss of bass, muddy sound, blocking
Paper-in-Oil Capacitors Tone Circuit Modern film capacitors (polyester/polypropylene) Leakage, noise, tone control failure
Carbon Comp Resistors Throughout Circuit Metal film resistors (where appropriate) Noise, value drift, thermal instability

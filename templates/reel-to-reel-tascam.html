TASCAM Models We Service

TASCAM 58

The ultimate project studio workhorse
  • Format: 1/2" 8-Track
  • Speed: 15 ips
  • Common Issues: Capstan motor, power supply
  • Service Expertise: Full calibration & alignment

TASCAM 34/32

The default stereo mixdown machine
  • Format: 1/4" 2-Track
  • Speed: 7.5/15 ips
  • Common Issues: Pinch roller wear, electronics
  • Service Expertise: Head alignment & replacement

TASCAM 80-8

Pioneering home multitrack recorder
  • Format: 1/2" 8-Track
  • Speed: 15 ips
  • Common Issues: Transport mechanism, old capacitors
  • Service Expertise: Complete overhaul available

TASCAM 38

High-end mixdown and broadcast machine
  • Format: 1/4" 2-Track
  • Speed: 7.5/15 ips
  • Common Issues: Brake system, relay contacts
  • Service Expertise: Precision speed calibration

TASCAM ATR-60

Professional studio flagship machine
  • Format: 1", 1/2" Multitrack
  • Speed: 15/30 ips
  • Common Issues: Complex transport electronics
  • Service Expertise: Full professional calibration

TASCAM 85 Series

Precursor to the ATR-60 series
  • Format: 1/2" 16-Track, 2" 24-Track
  • Speed: 15/30 ips
  • Common Issues: Aging logic boards, power supply
  • Service Expertise: Component-level diagnostics

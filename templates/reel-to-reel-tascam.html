TASCAM Models We Service

TASCAM 58

The ultimate project studio workhorse
  • Format: 1/2" 8-Track
  • Speed: 15 ips
  • Common Issues: Capstan motor, power supply
  • Service Expertise: Full calibration & alignment

TASCAM 34/32

The default stereo mixdown machine
  • Format: 1/4" 2-Track
  • Speed: 7.5/15 ips
  • Common Issues: Pinch roller wear, electronics
  • Service Expertise: Head alignment & replacement

TASCAM 80-8

Pioneering home multitrack recorder
  • Format: 1/2" 8-Track
  • Speed: 15 ips
  • Common Issues: Transport mechanism, old capacitors
  • Service Expertise: Complete overhaul available

TASCAM 38

High-end mixdown and broadcast machine
  • Format: 1/4" 2-Track
  • Speed: 7.5/15 ips
  • Common Issues: Brake system, relay contacts
  • Service Expertise: Precision speed calibration

TASCAM ATR-60

Professional studio flagship machine
  • Format: 1", 1/2" Multitrack
  • Speed: 15/30 ips
  • Common Issues: Complex transport electronics
  • Service Expertise: Full professional calibration

TASCAM 85 Series

Precursor to the ATR-60 series
  • Format: 1/2" 16-Track, 2" 24-Track
  • Speed: 15/30 ips
  • Common Issues: Aging logic boards, power supply
  • Service Expertise: Component-level diagnostics

Common questions

Does Tek Media Group repair TASCAM reel-to-reel machines?
Yes. Tek Media Group repairs and restores TASCAM machines at 711 South Victory Blvd, Burbank, CA, and accepts units shipped from anywhere in the United States. Models covered on this site include TASCAM 34/32, TASCAM 38, TASCAM 58, TASCAM 80-8, TASCAM 85 Series, TASCAM ATR-60.
How do I start a repair?
Start a service ticket online or call (818) 244-4440. Weekend and after-hours visits are by appointment.
What work does a restoration include?
Typical work on these machines includes capacitor replacement (recapping), belt, roller and bearing service, mechanical overhaul, head alignment and electronic calibration, depending on what the machine needs.

Send it in or bring it by appointment. Tell us what it is doing and we will take it from there.

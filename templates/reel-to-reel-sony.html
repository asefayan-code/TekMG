Common questions

Does Tek Media Group repair Sony reel-to-reel machines?
Yes. Tek Media Group repairs and restores Sony machines at 711 South Victory Blvd, Burbank, CA, and accepts units shipped from anywhere in the United States. Models covered on this site include Sony TC-788-4, Sony TC-880-2.
How do I start a repair?
Start a service ticket online or call (818) 244-4440. Weekend and after-hours visits are by appointment.
What work does a restoration include?
Typical work on these machines includes capacitor replacement (recapping), belt, roller and bearing service, mechanical overhaul, head alignment and electronic calibration, depending on what the machine needs.

Need your Sony repaired?

Send it in or bring it by appointment. Tell us what it is doing and we will take it from there. Pickup and delivery from San Francisco to San Diego.

Troubleshooting

Symptom & solution.

SymptomRepair solution
CD not reading or skippingClean or replace the laser lens assembly
Glass door or sledge stuckClean the IR sensors and mechanical rails
Sledge won't moveCheck the drive transistors
“Cleaning required” noticeThoroughly clean all sensors, lenses and mirrors

Handle with care

  • The components are precise, so disassembly takes great care.
  • Never move the sledge by hand while the unit is powered.
  • A “CLEANING REQUIRED” message can persist until the optical block is fully cleaned and aligned.

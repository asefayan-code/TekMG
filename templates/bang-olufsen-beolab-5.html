What We Do

Common BeoLab 5 repairs.

01

Amplifier & ICEpower

Red protection light, dead channel or no sound: we diagnose the amplifier protection circuit and replace the ICEpower amplifier or damaged output stage.

02

Acoustic lens repair

Acoustic lens mechanism repair or replacement of the lens assembly, with the tweeter realigned.

03

Drivers & reconing

Blown woofer or no bass: driver reconing and replacement, bass amplifier module repair, crossover check.

04

DSP & bass control

Woofer amplifier and DSP board faults, EEPROM failures and Adaptive Bass Control microphone faults.

Troubleshooting

Symptom & solution.

SymptomRepair solution
Red LED or error indicatorDiagnose and repair the amplifier protection circuit; replace the damaged output stage
No bass responseReplace the blown woofer; repair the bass amplifier module; check the crossover
One speaker not workingCheck the master and slave link cable; ICEpower amplifier module; power distribution
Acoustic lens problemsRepair or replace the acoustic lens assembly; realign the tweeter
Crackling or distortionRecone drivers; clean the voice coil; check connections
Physical damageRepair a broken acoustic lens, dented grille or damaged finish

Handle with care

  • BeoLab 5 contains active, mains-connected amplification. Never open one without proper ESD protection.
  • The ICEpower amplifier modules are proprietary and need specialized replacement.
  • Many internal repairs need B&O service tools, which is why this work belongs with an authorized service center.

TEK Media Group in Burbank, CA repairs and restores Luxman amplifiers, receivers, vacuum tube amps, CD players and turntables, from 1970s classics to recent models. Drop off at our shop or ship from anywhere in the U.S. Estimates are free.

Common Luxman problems we fix

  • No sound or one channel deadProtection relay stays open, failed output or driver transistors, or a dead channel on the amp board.
  • Crackling, scratchy volume or selectorWorn or oxidized volume controls, input selectors and switches, cleaned or replaced.
  • Hum, buzz or distortionAged power supply capacitors, drifting bias or DC offset, and cold solder joints.
  • Recap and full restorationElectrolytic capacitor replacement, bias and DC offset set to spec, relay and contact service, and a full bench test.
  • Tube amplifiersTube testing and replacement, bias adjustment and power supply service for Luxman SQ, MQ, LX and CL tube gear.
  • Turntables and CD playersSpeed and motor problems on Luxman turntables; tray, laser and drive faults on CD and SACD players.

How it works

  1. Start a service ticket online or call us with your model and the problem.
  2. Drop the unit off in Burbank, or pack it well (original box or plenty of padding) and ship it insured.
  3. We diagnose it and send you a free estimate before any work starts.
  4. After repair, every unit is bench tested before it goes back to you.

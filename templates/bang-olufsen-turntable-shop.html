Beogram 4002 and 4004 Restoration Program

Electronic components

  • Replace aging electrolytic capacitors where necessary (some specialists replace all).
  • Replace worn or oxidized potentiometers.
  • Replace defective electronic components.
  • Repair deteriorated wiring and solder connections.
  • Replace defective relays and sensors where necessary.

Mechanical components

  • Replace platter drive belt.
  • Replace tonearm drive belt, on applicable versions.
  • Replace worn pulleys and transport bushings.
  • Clean and lubricate motor bearings.
  • Clean and lubricate the tonearm mechanism.
  • Calibrate the tracking and arm movement.

Overview

📌 Key Features: Shares B77 transport and mechanics • Three-motor drive • 10.5" reel capacity • Modular PC board construction • XLR connectors (some versions) • Professional cosmetics

Due to its rarity, documentation is limited. Many PR77 restoration techniques are adapted from the B77 and PR99 service manuals, as the underlying mechanics and electronics are nearly identical.

Common Failure Modes

#1 RIFA Capacitor Failure (Mains/Suppression)

Symptom: Smoke, burning smell, loud cracking, blown fuses, tripped breakers. The "knallfrosch" (exploding frog) is infamous in all Revox machines of this era.

Cause: Paper dielectric absorbs moisture over decades and shorts catastrophically.

Repair: Replace ALL RIFA capacitors on sight before applying power. Located on mains input, relay board, and motor boards. Often accompanied by burnt series resistors.

#2 Philips "Blue" Electrolytic Capacitors

Symptom: Leaking brown goo, corroded PCB traces, lifted pads, audio distortion, motor control instability.

Cause: 1970s Philips capacitors have electrolyte formulation issues and leak destructively.

Repair: Replace ALL Philips blue electrolytics on every board. Inspect for trace corrosion underneath.

#3 Tantalum Capacitor Failure

Symptom: Short circuits, logic glitches, no function, destroyed ICs and transistors.

Cause: Tantalum beads fail shorted, taking out surrounding components.

Repair: Replace all tantalum capacitors on audio and logic boards with modern electrolytics or higher-voltage tantalums.

#4 Motherboard Switch Contact Oxidation

Symptom: Intermittent channel operation, controls not working, crackling sounds.

Cause: Despite gold-plated contacts, oxidation and corrosion can start on the surface after decades of storage.

Repair: Remove motherboard, clean all gold contacts on both motherboard and switch backs with DeOxIt-loaded Q-tip.

#5 Toggle Switch Breakage

Symptom: Switch shears off, won't stay in position, intermittent operation.

Cause: One of the weak points of Revox 10" machines - toggle switches can shear off if too much pressure is put on them, especially sideways.

Repair: Replace with new switches or repair using Nagravox repair kits.

#6 Motor Run Capacitor Failure

Symptom: Motors run hot, won't start, slow speed, capstan instability.

Cause: Motor capacitors degrade over time (typical values: capstan 3.5µF, reel motors 6.5+1.5µF).

Repair: Replace with new AC-rated motor run capacitors. Need slim 30mm diameter type due to space constraints.

#7 Press Solenoid Not Engaging

Symptom: When pressing play, the press solenoid does not move, but FF and RW work.

Cause: Transport keys may be contaminated, or cable harness faulty.

Repair: Test transport keys with multimeter in continuity mode (deck OFF). Check grey wire (common) through each button: pause, rewind, f.fwd, stop, play, rec.

#8 Reel Motor Bearings

Symptom: Grinding noise, rough rotation, excessive wow/flutter.

Cause: Dried lubricant, worn bearings from years of use.

Repair: Replace bearings with precision low-noise bearings (SKF 608-2Z or equivalent) including circlips.

#9 Guide Roller and Tension Arm Damping

Symptom: Sloppy tape path, inconsistent tension, wowing.

Cause: Bearings wear, damper fluid dries out over decades.

Repair: Replace guide roller bearings, repack tension arm dampers with fresh drag agent (300,000 cSt silicon fluid).

#10 Brake Lining Wear

Symptom: Tape spillage, uneven wind, machine won't stop properly.

Cause: Felt brake bands wear or become contaminated.

Repair: Replace brake linings, adjust per service manual.

#11 Pinch Roller Hardening

Symptom: Slipping, wow/flutter, tape speed issues.

Cause: Rubber hardens with age.

Repair: Replace with new pinch roller including spacers and shims.

#12 VU Meter Bulb Failure

Symptom: Meters not illuminated, intermittent lighting.

Cause: Original 24V bulbs burn out; oxidation on bulb socket can cause intermittent operation.

Repair: Replace with E85 28V bulb for longer life. Rotate bulb 180 degrees if oxidation is suspected.

⚠️ CRITICAL: Motherboard Switch Contact Cleaning

THE #1 FIX FOR INTERMITTENT OPERATION

One of the most common problems with the PR77 (and B77/PR99) is intermittent channel operation caused by oxidized contacts between the motherboard and the toggle/rotary switches.

Cleaning Procedure:

  1. Remove back cover (4 screws) and bottom PC board shield (2 screws)
  2. Pull all audio PC boards - TAKE PICTURES of orientation and order
  3. Remove motherboard (12 Philips screws) - careful with long speed switch attached to white plastic lever
  4. Unplug white/black and green/black wire pairs near top of motherboard
  5. Tilt motherboard down about 90 degrees to access back of board
  6. Load Q-tip with DeOxIt D5 until almost dripping
  7. Gently wipe down ALL gold contacts on motherboard
  8. Clean backs of toggle and rotary switch contacts with same method
  9. You'll be surprised how much crud comes off even on clean-looking contacts
  10. Repeat until no more crud comes off
  11. Reassemble ensuring all plastic switch standoffs fit flush through motherboard
🔧 CRITICAL NOTES:
  • The long speed switch has a rectangular slot that fits into a white tab - wiggle carefully to avoid breaking
  • Ensure wires route through round cutouts and aren't pinched between motherboard and chassis
  • Plastic standoffs MUST be flush with component side of motherboard for proper contact
  • Install all 12 screws and ensure they're tight - need solid connection

Mechanical Overhaul Guide

Reel Motor Bearings:

  • Replace with 4x precision low-noise bearings (SKF 608-2Z or equivalent)
  • Includes circlips for proper retention
  • Critical for quiet operation and low wow/flutter

Capstan Motor Lubrication:

  • Use special Klueber PDP65 capstan motor lubricant
  • Essential for proper servo lock and speed stability

Guide Roller Overhaul:

  • Tape guide overhaul kit with stainless steel roller bearings
  • Includes many shims for proper alignment

Tension Arm Damper Service:

  • Special damper repacking lubricant / drag agent (300,000 cSt)
  • Clean old dried fluid completely before repacking

Brake Service:

  • Replace brake linings (felt material)
  • Full brake overhaul option includes brake bands and associated fittings
  • Adjust for proper tape tension per service manual

Pinch Roller Replacement:

  • New pinch roller including spacers and shims
  • Shims for pinch roller arm height adjustment

General Lubrication:

  • General lubricant for levers, shafts and bushes
  • Counter belts as applicable

Transport Key Testing Procedure

If your PR77's play solenoid doesn't engage but FF/RW work, test the transport keys with a multimeter:

Continuity Test (Deck OFF):

  1. Locate transport key connector on tape drive PCB (left side from behind)
  2. Contacts available from top of PCB
  3. Place one lead on first contact (grey wire - common +24V)
  4. Second lead on each contact in order:
    • Second contact: pause
    • Third: rewind
    • Fourth: f.fwd
    • Fifth: stop
    • Sixth: play
    • Seventh: rec (press play simultaneously)
  5. Should read continuity when button pressed
  6. If no continuity, problem is switches or wire harness

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • ❌ Powering up without replacing RIFA caps: Explosive failure, smoke, fire
  • ❌ Skipping mechanical restoration: Recap alone isn't enough - bearings, dampers, brakes need attention
  • ❌ Breaking the speed switch: When removing motherboard, the long speed switch sticks to white lever - wiggle carefully
  • ❌ Pinching wires: Easy to pinch wires between motherboard and chassis during reassembly
  • ❌ Improper motherboard seating: Plastic standoffs MUST be flush with motherboard for proper contact
  • ❌ Forgetting ground wire orientation: Ground wire must be routed correctly or front cover won't fit
  • ❌ Not documenting disassembly: Take pictures of board order and connector positions
  • ❌ Using wrong bulb: VU meter bulb is 24V, replace with E85 28V for longer life

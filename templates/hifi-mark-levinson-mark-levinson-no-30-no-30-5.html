About the Mark Levinson No. 30 / 30.5 & Our Approach

The Mark Levinson No. 30 and No. 30.5 (the latter with HDCD decoding) are legendary reference CD processors from the golden age of digital audio (1992-1999). The No. 30 set new standards for CD playback, featuring a dual-chassis design (transport and DAC separate), Philips CDM-9 Pro or CDM-12 Pro transport, dual PCM63P-K DACs, and a massive power supply. Known for their incredible musicality, low jitter, and robust build, these units remain highly sought after. We've restored dozens of Mark Levinson digital components over three decades, and we treat each unit with meticulous care, using premium replacement parts while preserving the original sonic character.

Deep Dive: Our Mark Levinson No. 30/30.5 Restoration Process

⚡ POWER SUPPLY (Critical for Low Noise)
  • Main Filter Capacitors: Large snap-in electrolytics in the DAC and transport chassis – we replace with high-ripple, low-ESR, 105°C rated types (Nichicon KG, Panasonic, or Cornell Dubilier).
  • Regulator Section: We replace all small electrolytic capacitors on the regulator boards, verify voltage rails (±15V, +5V, +5VA, etc.) within tolerance.
  • Bridge Rectifiers: We test and replace if noisy, upgrade to ultra-fast soft recovery types for lower noise.
  • AC Input & Safety: We inspect AC input filter, replace RIFA caps if present, check fuses and IEC inlet.
🔄 PHILIPS CDM-9 PRO / CDM-12 PRO TRANSPORT OVERHAUL
COMPLETE MECHANICAL & OPTICAL SERVICE:
  • Disassembly & Cleaning: We fully disassemble the transport, clean old lubricants, and regrease sled mechanism and spindle motor bearings.
  • Belt Replacement: The tray belt and sled belt are replaced with factory-spec or upgraded equivalents.
  • Spindle Motor Check: We test spindle motor for noise and runout, replace if necessary (common failure).
  • Laser Pickup Assessment: Using an oscilloscope, we measure the HF ("eye pattern") amplitude and jitter. We adjust laser power only if needed, or recommend replacement for worn pickups.
  • Focus & Tracking Calibration: We adjust focus offset, tracking gain, and focus gain using a test CD and oscilloscope to factory specifications.
  • CDM-12 Pro Specific: We address the common issue of the magnetic puck and spindle hub, ensure proper clamping.
🎛️ DIGITAL BOARD & SERVO ELECTRONICS
  • Servo Board Capacitors: All electrolytic capacitors on the Philips CD servo board are replaced (typically 10-25 units).
  • Decoupling Capacitors: We add or upgrade local decoupling on digital ICs to reduce noise.
  • Clock Circuit: We verify the master clock (11.2896MHz or similar) for accuracy and jitter, replace crystal if drifted.
  • DF1700 Digital Filter: We check power supply to the DF1700 filter, ensure clean decoupling.
  • PCM63P-K DAC Section: We verify reference currents, check we/V converter op-amps, replace capacitors in the analog path.
🔊 ANALOG OUTPUT STAGE RESTORATION
  • Coupling Capacitors: We replace electrolytic output coupling caps with high-quality film or bipolar audio-grade types (Nichicon ES, Elna Silmic II).
  • Op-Amp Upgrade (Optional): Original op-amps (typically NE5534/OPA627) can be upgraded to modern low-noise types (OPA1611, OPA1656) if desired.
  • Power Supply Decoupling: We add or replace local decoupling capacitors near each op-amp.
  • DC Offset Check: We verify and adjust DC offset at outputs to near 0mV.
  • HDCD Decoder (No. 30.5 specific): We check the PMD100 HDCD decoder operation, replace capacitors in its power supply.

Common Mark Levinson No. 30/30.5 Problems we Solve

#1 Transport Read Errors / Skipping

Symptom: Disc not recognized, skipping, clicking, failure to read TOC.
Our Solution: Clean/lubricate transport, replace belts, adjust laser focus/tracking, replace spindle motor if worn, replace laser pickup if necessary.

#2 Capacitor Aging in Power Supply

Symptom: Increased noise, hum, intermittent operation, poor bass response.
Our Solution: Complete recap of all electrolytic capacitors in both chassis (power supply, digital, and analog boards).

#3 No Audio Output / Distorted Audio

Symptom: One or both channels dead, distorted sound, low output.
Our Solution: Check DAC reference voltages, replace failed op-amps, recap analog stage, verify relay operation.

#4 No Lock / Unstable Lock on Digital Inputs

Symptom: Input selector doesn't lock, intermittent playback.
Our Solution: Check digital input receiver (CS8412/CS8414), replace capacitors in PLL circuit.

#5 Display Issues (Dim or Missing)

Symptom: VFD display dim or not working.
Our Solution: Check filament voltage, replace driver capacitors, replace VFD if necessary (we have sources).

#6 Tray Mechanism Issues

Symptom: Tray won't open/close, grinding noise.
Our Solution: Replace tray belt, clean and lubricate gears, adjust tray position switches.

#7 Remote Control Not Working

Symptom: No response to remote.
Our Solution: Check IR receiver, replace electrolytic caps on front panel board.

Precision Calibration & Alignment Procedure

📀 OUR CALIBRATION PROCESS FOR NO. 30/30.5:
  • Laser Alignment: Using an oscilloscope and Philips test CD (SBC 444A), we adjust laser power, focus offset, tracking gain, and focus gain to factory specifications. The HF "eye pattern" should be clear with amplitude >1.2V p-p.
  • Spindle & Sled Servo: We verify and adjust spindle motor gain and sled servo for smooth, accurate tracking.
  • Clock Verification: We measure master clock frequency and jitter using a frequency counter and jitter analyzer, replacing crystal if off-spec.
  • DAC Calibration: For PCM63P-K, we verify the reference current (Iref) and adjust if necessary using trim pots.
  • Analog Stage: We measure frequency response, distortion (THD+N), and signal-to-noise ratio, aiming for original specifications: THD+N <0.002%, S/N >110dB.
  • Digital Inputs: We test all digital inputs (AES/EBU, SPDIF, Toslink) for proper lock and low jitter.

Frequently Asked Questions – Mark Levinson No. 30/30.5

❓ What's the difference between No. 30 and No. 30.5?

The No. 30.5 adds HDCD decoding (PMD100 chip) and has some circuit refinements. Both are reference-grade processors. We service both models with equal expertise.

❓ What transport mechanism does it use?

Early units used Philips CDM-9 Pro (swing-arm), later units used CDM-12 Pro. Both are excellent but require specialized service. We work on both.

1970s "Silver Face" Era - Vintage Receiver & Tuner Restoration

The legendary silver-face era represents the pinnacle of classic Marantz design and engineering. These receivers and tuners are highly sought after by collectors and audiophiles worldwide. We provide full recap, alignment, power supply restoration, and LED bulb replacement.

Marantz Model 19 Receiver
Stereo Receiver / Integrated Amplifier-Tuner
Years: 1970–1975
Expert Model 19 Restoration: Unique "receiver-plus" design with separate preamp, power amp, and tuner sections in one chassis. Known for its massive build quality and warm, tube-like solid-state sound. Our service includes complete electrolytic capacitor replacement, power supply rebuild, tuner alignment (including the legendary Marantz RF front end), and full transistor testing. Common issues we address: noisy selector switches, drifting FM, aging power supply caps, and intermittent output relay. Sought after by serious collectors.
Marantz Model 2230 Receiver
Stereo Receiver
Years: 1973–1976
Model 2230 Specialist Service: 30-watt per channel classic, famous for its exceptional phono stage and warm "Midwest sound." Perfect for smaller listening rooms. Our restoration includes full recap using high-quality audio-grade capacitors, bias and offset adjustment, tuner alignment, and LED lamp upgrade (optional). Common fixes: scratchy volume pots, dead dial lights, and intermittent protection circuit. A favorite among vintage enthusiasts.
Marantz Model 2270 Receiver
Stereo Receiver
Years: 1971–1975
Legendary Model 2270 Restoration – The Holy Grail: 70-watt receiver that defined the silver-face era. Renowned for its authoritative bass, detailed midrange, and beautiful blue dial. Our comprehensive service includes full recap of power supply and audio boards, replacement of known noisy transistors (2SC458, 2SC871), FM alignment with oscilloscope, and relay/switch rebuild. We solve: hum in one channel, weak FM stereo, intermittent audio, and high DC offset. Fully restored 2270s are prized by audiophiles worldwide.
Marantz Model 2325 Receiver
Stereo Receiver
Years: 1974–1977
Model 2325 Professional Service: 125-watt per channel monster, featuring dual power supplies and massive heat sinks. One of the most powerful receivers Marantz ever produced. Our restoration includes full power supply capacitor replacement, output transistor matching, bias recalibration, and tuner section alignment. Common issues we fix: overheating, protection mode, burned lamps, and worn speaker relays. A true powerhouse for serious listeners.
Marantz Model 30 Receiver
Stereo Receiver
Years: 1975–1978
Model 30 Restoration – Underrated Classic: 45-watt receiver with exceptional build quality and a distinctive "tilt face" design. It shares circuitry with higher-end models but is more affordable. Our service includes full capacitor replacement, tuner alignment, and cleaning of all controls. Known issues: noisy preamp stage, dim tuner dial, and intermittent headphone jack. An excellent entry point into vintage Marantz ownership.
Marantz Model 33 Receiver
Stereo Receiver
Years: 1968–1972
Early Solid-State Gem – Model 33 Service: One of the first solid-state receivers from Marantz, featuring a unique oscilloscope display (where equipped). Our specialized restoration includes rebuilding the power supply, replacing all electrolytic and tantalum capacitors, oscilloscope calibration (if present), and full transistor testing. Common repairs: failing scope tube, distorted audio, weak FM sensitivity. Rare and collectible – we provide museum-quality restoration.

Modern & High-End Marantz Service

Beyond vintage, TEK Media Group is fully equipped to service modern Marantz high-end components: SACD players, reference integrated amplifiers, and KI Signature series.

Marantz Model 10 (PM-10) Integrated Amplifier
Reference Integrated Amplifier
Years: 2018–2024
PM-10 Specialist Repair – Modern Flagship: Dual-mono, fully balanced design with 200W per channel. Years: 2008–2014
SA-7S3 Precision Service: High-end disc player with proprietary DAC and analog output stage. SACD / CD Player & USB DAC

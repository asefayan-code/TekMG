Projector Repair FAQ

Do you repair projectors in Burbank, CA?

Yes. Tek Media Group at 711 South Victory Blvd, Burbank, CA 91502 repairs and cleans projectors, including LCD, DLP, LED, 4K, home theater, business and educational models. Call (818) 244-4440.

Which projector brands do you service?

We service Epson, NEC, Sharp, JVC, Sony, Panasonic, BenQ, Barco, Optoma, Christie, Hitachi, ViewSonic, Canon, Mitsubishi, LG, Toshiba, XGIMI, InFocus, Eiki, Proxima, Dell, Sanyo, Marantz, Acer, Vivitek, Casio, Digital Projection, Runco and Ricoh projectors.

Are you an authorized Epson service provider?

We are a warranty service provider for Epson only. For all other brands we provide out-of-warranty repair and cleaning.

Do you charge for a projector estimate?

No. Diagnostics and estimates are free.

What warranty do you give on projector repairs?

Every repair we do carries a 90-day warranty.

What projector problems do you repair?

Common repairs include no picture or blank screen, overheating or loud fan noise, lamp replacement, color wheel and DMD chip repair, LCD panel and prism repair, power supply and ballast repair, lines or spots in the image, focus and lens problems, a projector that will not power on, and HDMI or input problems. We also clean the exterior, interior, cooling system and lens optics.

What are your hours?

Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM. Weekends, holidays and after hours are by appointment only. Call (818) 244-4440.

Can I bring in, mail or schedule pickup of my projector?

Yes. You can walk in at our Burbank shop, ship your projector to us from anywhere in the country, or use our pickup and delivery service from San Francisco to San Diego. Start a service ticket online or call (818) 244-4440.

Most Common Failures in Model 10B

Primary Failure Points

  • Electrolytic Capacitors - The primary concern in these vintage units. They dry out, lose capacitance, and can leak.
  • Power Supply Components - The regulated power supply is critical to proper operation.
  • Tuning Capacitors - The famous 6-gang tuning capacitor can develop issues with age.
  • Tube Sockets and Connections - Oxidation and poor connections can cause various problems.
  • Bulbs and Lamps - The stereo indicator and dial lights often need replacement.

Capacitor Replacement Tips for Model 10B

A careful "recap" is essential for a Model 10B to perform to its original specifications. Extreme caution is needed with alignment.

Power Supply Section - HIGH PRIORITY

The regulated power supply is critical to the performance of the Model 10B.

Reference Original Value Replacement Suggestions
C901, C902 40µF / 450V Main filter caps. Use high-quality replacements of exact value.
C903 20µF / 450V Filter capacitor. Match original value precisely.
C904, C905 20µF / 25V Low-voltage filters. Nichicon UPW or similar.
C906 40µF / 25V Low-voltage filter. Match capacitance value.
Amplifier and Detector Boards

These boards contain critical alignment components. Take photos before replacing anything.

Reference Original Value Replacement Suggestions
C101-C108 Various values RF circuit caps. Replace with same type and value.
C201-C208 Various values IF circuit caps. Must match original specifications.
C301-C310 Various values Detector circuit. Critical for proper stereo decoding.
Audio Output Section

Less critical for alignment but important for sound quality.

Reference Original Value Replacement Suggestions
C401, C402 0.1µF / 400V Output coupling caps. High-quality film capacitors recommended.
C403, C404 0.047µF / 400V Tone circuit caps. Film type preferred.

Additional Tips for Model 10B

  • Alignment Critical: The Model 10B has complex alignment procedures. Don't adjust any coils or transformers without proper equipment and knowledge.
  • Tube Replacement: Consider replacing all tubes with known good ones after recapping.
  • Cleaning: Carefully clean the tuning capacitor plates with appropriate solvents.
  • Documentation: Take detailed photos before disassembling anything.
  • Specialist Recommended: The Model 10B is a complex instrument best serviced by experts.

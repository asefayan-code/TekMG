Revox C270 Restoration

Professional Studio Tape Recorder • 1986-1993 • The Digital-Ready Analog Master

DISCLAIMER: This information is provided for educational purposes only. While we strive for accuracy, we cannot guarantee all information is correct or complete. Always consult official Revox documentation when available. Proceed at your own risk.
⚠️ IMMEDIATE SAFETY WARNING: The Revox C270 contains RIFA "explosive" paper capacitors that WILL fail catastrophically with smoke and fire. Never apply mains power to an unrestored C270 without first replacing all RIFA capacitors. Many machines have had hard lives in studios and radio stations, requiring both electronic and mechanical restoration.

Overview

The Revox C270 is the professional successor to the B77/PR99 series, produced from approximately 1986 to 1993. It features three-motor drive with electronic control, vari-speed from -33% to +50%, real-time tape counter, and exceptional specs (0.05% wow/flutter at 38 cm/s). Available in both 2-track and 4-track versions with butterfly heads, it represents the pinnacle of Revox analog engineering before the digital era.

📌 Key Specifications: Speeds: 9.5/19/38 cm/s • Wow/Flutter: <0.05% at 38 cm/s • Dynamics: >65dB at 38 cm/s • Mass: 23kg • Real-time counter with Zero Locator • Three electronically controlled motors.

Common Failure Modes

#1 RIFA "Explosive" Capacitors (Mains/Suppression)

Symptom: Smoke, burning smell, loud cracking, blown fuses, tripped breakers. One forum member reported finding a cracked RIFA that needed immediate replacement.

Cause: Paper dielectric absorbs moisture over decades and shorts catastrophically.

Repair: Replace ALL RIFA capacitors on sight before applying power. Located on mains input, motor boards, and across motor run capacitors. Some units have "undocumented" 0.47µF 275VAC X2 caps across supply motor run capacitors.

#2 Motor Run Capacitor Failure

Symptom: Motors run hot, won't start, slow speed, capstan instability. One user reported a physically broken motor capacitor as the cause of slow playback.

Cause: Motor capacitors (typically 8µF for each motor) degrade over time. The C270 needs slim 30mm diameter replacements due to space constraints.

Repair: Replace with new AC-rated motor run capacitors. Check value per schematic (8µF for both motors on some versions).

#3 MOSFET Motor Drive Failure (Q25, Q26)

Symptom: Motor runs continuously at power-on, brakes not releasing, no response to transport buttons.

Cause: FET Q25 (IRF730/IRF733) fails shorted, often taking out surrounding components. One detailed repair thread documented repeated FET failures until diodes and motor caps were replaced.

Repair: Replace with IRF730PBF or equivalent. Check diodes D26-D29, bridge rectifier DZ1, and motor capacitors. Verify no short circuits before power-up.

#4 Tension Arm Calibration Drift

Symptom: Machine won't stop when tape ends, runs wild in rewind, functions work with no tape present.

Cause: The 4V reference settings drift, affecting comparator IC8 and the TAPE OUT signal to CPU pin 21. Stray capacitances from missing front panel affect calibration due to 1.228 MHz excitation frequency.

Repair: Recalibrate tension arm electronics per service manual. Critical: front panel MUST be in place during calibration. Adjust both 4V settings precisely.

#5 Motor Bearing Wear (Studio Use)

Symptom: Noisy motors, grinding sounds, excessive rumble.

Cause: Many C270s had hard lives in recording studios and radio stations, causing bearing wear.

Repair: Replace with new SKF bearings (typically 608-2Z) and appropriate lubricant. Complete motor overhaul kit available including bearings, circlips, and lubricants.

#6 Three Different Capstan Motor Types

Symptom: Incorrect lubrication can destroy motor.

Cause: C270 uses three different capstan motors requiring different lubricants.

Repair: Identify motor type by sticker:

  • Early motors: PDP65 oil (white sticker/no sticker)
  • Later motors: GLY2100 thick oil/semi-grease (clear red sticker)
  • Latest motors: Roller bearing - NO lubrication (large red sticker)

#7 Guide Roller Bearings & Tension Dampers

Symptom: Sloppy tape path, inconsistent tension, wowing.

Cause: Bearings wear, damper fluid dries out over decades.

Repair: Replace guide roller bearings, repack tension arm dampers with fresh drag agent (300,000 cSt silicon fluid).

#8 Brake Lining Wear

Symptom: Tape spillage, uneven wind, machine won't stop properly.

Cause: Felt brake bands wear or become contaminated.

Repair: Replace brake linings, adjust per service manual.

#9 4050 Logic IC Failure (IC21)

Symptom: Motor control issues, PWM drive problems, excessive FET dissipation.

Cause: 4050 buffer IC degrades rise/fall times of PWM square waves.

Repair: Replace IC21 (4050) if rise/fall times are degraded. Inexpensive preventive replacement.

#10 Tantalum Capacitor Failures

Symptom: Short circuits, logic glitches, power supply issues.

Cause: Tantalum beads fail shorted, especially in professional use.

Repair: Replace all tantalum capacitors during restoration.

#11 VU Meter Lamp Failure

Symptom: Meters not illuminated.

Cause: Original lamps burn out after decades.

Repair: Replace with 2x VU meter globes from restoration kit.

#12 Pinch Roller Hardening

Symptom: Slipping, wow/flutter, tape speed issues.

Cause: Rubber hardens with age.

Repair: Replace with new pinch roller, including PTFE washers and metal shims.

⚠️ CRITICAL: Capstan Motor Identification

INCORRECT LUBRICATION WILL DESTROY YOUR MOTOR

The C270 uses three different capstan motors requiring different lubricants. Identify your motor type BEFORE attempting any lubrication:

Motor Type Identification Lubricant Required
Early Motors No label, or small white sticker may say PDP65 (often fallen off) PDP65 oil (light oil)
Later Motors Clear red sticker GLY2100 (thick oil/semi-grease)
Latest Motors Large red sticker clearly stating motor type NO LUBRICATION REQUIRED - roller bearing type
🔧 For roller bearing motors: DO NOT disassemble nor squirt oil in these motors. If motor is noisy, contact specialist for advice.

Systematic Restoration Process

🔧 RESTORATION PHILOSOPHY: The C270 requires both electronic AND mechanical restoration. Many machines had hard studio lives. Don't think that just doing a recap is all that's required.

"Full Monty" Restoration Checklist:

✅ Stage 0 - Documentation (photos of all connectors and wiring)
✅ Stage 1 - Replace ALL RIFA capacitors (safety critical)
✅ Stage 2 - Replace motor run capacitors (slim 30mm diameter)
✅ Stage 3 - Replace all polar electrolytic capacitors
✅ Stage 4 - Replace all tantalum capacitors
✅ Stage 5 - Replace open-type trimmer potentiometers
✅ Stage 6 - Motor overhaul: bearings, circlips, correct lubricant
✅ Stage 7 - Replace pinch roller with PTFE washers and shims
✅ Stage 8 - Right tape guide bearing kit
✅ Stage 9 - Left tensioner damper repack with fresh drag agent
✅ Stage 10 - Guide rollers overhaul, fresh soft guide bands
✅ Stage 11 - Brake lining replacement
✅ Stage 12 - General lubrication of levers, shafts, bushes
✅ Stage 13 - Reel table height shims adjustment
✅ Stage 14 - Replace VU meter lamps
✅ Stage 15 - Full calibration with test tape

Important: Some C270s have undocumented components (like extra RIFA caps across motor run capacitors) that must be located and replaced.

Mechanical Overhaul Guide

Motor Overhaul:

  • Replace reel motor bearings with new SKF bearings
  • Capstan motor lubrication - identify type FIRST (PDP65, GLY2100, or none)
  • Replace circlips during disassembly
  • Clean old lubricant thoroughly

Guide Roller Bearings:

  • Not all models have guide roller bearings - check your machine
  • Replace if noisy or rough
  • Part of full mechanical kit

Tension Arm Damper Service:

  1. Disassemble tension arm
  2. Clean old dried drag agent
  3. Repack with fresh damping fluid (300,000 cSt silicon oil)
  4. Replace seals if damaged
  5. Test for proper damping action

Brake Service:

  • Replace brake linings
  • Adjust for proper tape tension per service manual
  • Check for even application

Pinch Roller Replacement:

  • Use new original Revox pinch roller if available
  • Includes low-friction PTFE washers
  • Metal shims for proper alignment

Reel Table Height:

  • Check and adjust with shims
  • Critical for proper tape winding

General Lubrication:

  • All levers, shafts, and bushes
  • Use appropriate light oils and greases
  • Don't over-lubricate

Calibration Essentials

Tension Arm Calibration (CRITICAL):

  1. Front panel MUST be in place during calibration due to 1.228 MHz excitation frequency and stray capacitances
  2. Adjust both 4V reference settings precisely
  3. Verify comparator switching at IC8 (pins 1,2,3)
  4. Check CPU pin 21 changes state when tensioner nears lower position
  5. If settings drifted to ~6V, recalibration is essential

Full Electronic Calibration:

  • Requires test tape (IEC/NAB appropriate for your version)
  • Oscilloscope for bias adjustment
  • Signal generator
  • AC millivoltmeter
  • Distortion analyzer (optional)
⚠️ IMPORTANT: Front panel must be in place for any tension-related adjustments. Stray capacitance affects readings.

Routine Maintenance (Per Manual)

The C270 requires no regular maintenance other than occasional cleaning and demagnetizing.

Cleaning:

  • Clean capstan shaft, pinch roller, soundheads, tape guides
  • Use REVOX cleaning kit (Part No. 39000) or equivalent
  • Moisten felt stick with cleaning fluid, clean all tape guidance elements
  • Dry with clean felt swab or cleaning web
  • Important: No cleaning fluid into capstan bearing or VU meter covers

Demagnetizing:

  • Use REVOX demagnetizing choke (Part No. 10.042.002.01)
  • Switch off recorder, remove tape sufficiently far away
  • Slowly approach part with energized choke, retract slowly
  • Treat all metal parts that contact tape: soundheads, guides, rollers, lifting pin
  • Withdraw choke ~50cm before switching off
  • Caution: Demagnetizer will erase tapes if too close

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Wrong capstan lubricant: Using PDP65 on roller-bearing motors destroys them. Identify motor type FIRST.
  • Calibrating tension without front panel: Stray capacitance gives wrong readings. Front panel MUST be installed.
  • Ignoring undocumented RIFAs: Some units have extra 0.47µF caps across motor run caps - find and replace them.
  • Replacing FET without checking diodes/caps: New FET will fail immediately if other parts bad.
  • Skipping mechanical restoration: Recap alone isn't enough - bearings, dampers, brakes need attention.
  • Using wrong motor caps: C270 needs slim 30mm diameter caps due to space constraints.

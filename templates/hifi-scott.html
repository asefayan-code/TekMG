About the H.H. Scott 355 & Our Approach

The H.H. Scott 355 is a legendary all-tube AM/FM stereo receiver from the golden age of hi-fi (circa 1962-1965). Known for its lush, warm sound, excellent tuner section, and robust construction, it's a favorite among collectors and vintage audio enthusiasts. The 355 features a sensitive FM tuner with a 6BL8 multiplex decoder, a capable preamp section, and a power amplifier using 7591 output tubes delivering about 35-40 watts per channel. We've restored countless Scott units over three decades, and we treat each one with the respect it deserves, using modern high-quality components while preserving the original sonic character.

Deep Dive: Our H.H. Scott 355 Restoration Process

⚡ POWER SUPPLY & SAFETY (Critical First Step)
  • Selenium Rectifier: We replace the original selenium rectifier (a common failure and toxic hazard) with a modern silicon bridge diode, adding a dropping resistor to maintain proper B+ voltage.
  • Multi-Section Filter Caps: The original "twist-lock" electrolytic capacitors are rebuilt or replaced with modern high-temperature, high-ripple units. We either restuff the cans or discreetly mount new capacitors for reliability and safety.
  • Bias Supply: We rebuild the negative bias supply with fresh capacitors and diodes, ensuring stable output tube bias and extended tube life.
  • Safety Modifications: We add a fused IEC power inlet, replace the two-prong cord with a grounded cord, and install safety caps across the AC line.
🎚️ AUDIO & COUPLING STAGES
COMPLETE SIGNAL PATH RESTORATION:
  • Coupling Capacitors: We replace all bumblebee, black beauty, and wax/paper capacitors with high-quality polypropylene film caps (Auricap, Illinois, or similar). This eliminates leakage, restores bass response, and eliminates distortion.
  • Cathode Bypass Caps: All electrolytic cathode bypass capacitors are replaced with low-impedance, high-reliability types.
  • Resistor Replacement: Carbon composition resistors drift high over time. We measure and replace any resistor out of tolerance (typically >10% drift) with carbon film or metal film for improved noise performance.
  • Tube Socket & Pin Cleaning: We clean all tube sockets and pins for reliable contact.
📻 FM TUNER & MULTIPLEX ALIGNMENT
  • Front End Check: We verify oscillator and RF section performance, replace weak tubes (6AQ8, 6AU6, etc.).
  • IF Strip Alignment: Using a sweep generator and oscilloscope, we perform a full 10.7MHz IF alignment to restore sensitivity and selectivity.
  • Multiplex Decoder (6BL8): We align the 38kHz oscillator, adjust separation and stereo blend for proper stereo performance.
  • FM Detector & Ratio Detector: We calibrate the discriminator for lowest distortion and correct center tuning.
💡 TUBE TESTING & OUTPUT SECTION
  • Tube Testing: We test every tube on a calibrated Hickok tube tester for mutual conductance and gas. Weak or microphonic tubes are identified and replaced.
  • Output Tube Matching (7591): The 7591 output tubes are critical. We match them for transconductance and bias current to ensure balanced operation and lowest distortion.
  • Bias Adjustment: We modify the bias circuit to include individual bias adjustments or matched pairs for optimal performance and tube longevity.
  • Phase Inverter Balance: We check and balance the phase inverter for symmetrical drive to the output tubes.

Common H.H. Scott 355 Problems we Solve

#1 Selenium Rectifier Failure

Symptom: No power, low B+, burning smell, hum.
Our Solution: Replace with silicon diodes, add dropping resistor, rebuild bias supply for reliability and safety.

#2 Leaky Bumblebee / Paper Capacitors

Symptom: Muddy sound, low volume, distortion, output tube red-plating.
Our Solution: We replace ALL paper and bumblebee caps with modern film caps, restoring proper frequency response and safety.

#3 Weak / No FM Reception

Symptom: Poor stereo separation, hiss, inability to tune stations.
Our Solution: Full FM alignment, tube replacement, and multiplex decoder service.

#4 Hum & Ripple

Symptom: Audible 60Hz or 120Hz hum.
Our Solution: Rebuild power supply filter caps, replace cathode bypass caps, check grounding, add or replace can capacitors.

#5 Noisy Controls / Scratchy Pots

Symptom: Crackling when adjusting volume or balance.
Our Solution: Thorough cleaning with DeoxIT, lubrication, replacement if necessary.

#6 Output Tube Red-Plating

Symptom: Plates glowing red, distortion, blown fuse.
Our Solution: Rebuild bias supply, replace coupling caps, match output tubes, add bias adjustment mod.

FM Tuner & Amplifier Calibration

📻 PRECISION ALIGNMENT & CALIBRATION:
  • FM IF Alignment: Using a sweep generator (10.7MHz) and oscilloscope, we align each IF transformer for maximum gain and correct bandwidth.
  • FM Detector / Ratio Detector: We adjust for minimum distortion and correct center tuning using a distortion analyzer.
  • Multiplex Decoder Alignment: We adjust the 38kHz oscillator and separation controls for maximum stereo separation (>30dB) and minimal crosstalk.
  • Amplifier Bias & Balance: We set bias current per channel and balance phase inverter for symmetrical output.
  • Power Output Check: We verify power output and distortion using an audio analyzer.

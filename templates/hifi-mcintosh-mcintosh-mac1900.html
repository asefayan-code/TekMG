About the McIntosh MAC 1900 & Our Service

The McIntosh MAC 1900 is a landmark solid-state receiver from the early 1970s, representing the pinnacle of McIntosh's receiver technology. Produced from 1972 to 1975, this receiver delivers 55 watts per channel and features a sensitive FM tuner with a 5-gang front end, built-in Dolby FM noise reduction, and McIntosh's legendary Autoformer output transformers. With its classic glass front panel, brass chassis, and unmistakable McIntosh styling, the MAC 1900 is highly sought after by collectors and audiophiles who want vintage McIntosh sound with solid-state reliability.

The McIntosh MAC 1900 – Solid-State Excellence

🎵 McINTOSH MAC 1900 KEY FEATURES:
  • Power Output: 55 watts per channel (solid-state)
  • Autoformer Outputs: McIntosh's legendary output transformers for stable 4/8/16 ohm operation
  • FM Tuner: Advanced 5-gang front end for superior sensitivity and selectivity
  • Dolby FM: Built-in Dolby FM noise reduction for quieter FM reception
  • Glass Front Panel: Classic McIntosh aesthetic with backlit glass and meters
  • Dual Meters: Signal strength and center tuning meters
  • High Filter: 7kHz high-cut filter for noisy FM reception
  • Stereo Beacon: Pilot lamp indicator for stereo reception
⚠️ IMPORTANT – MAC 1900 SPECIFIC CONSIDERATIONS: The MAC 1900 uses electrolytic and tantalum capacitors that are known to fail with age. Failed capacitors can cause noise, hum, and can damage output transistors. The Dolby FM circuitry uses specialized ICs and capacitors that must be properly restored for correct operation. We have the specific expertise and test equipment to service this advanced receiver.

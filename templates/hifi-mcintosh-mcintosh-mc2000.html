About the MC2000 & Our Service

The McIntosh MC2000 was released in 1999 to celebrate the company's 50th anniversary. Only 2,500 units were produced worldwide, making it one of the most collectible McIntosh amplifiers ever made. With its stunning stainless steel chassis, gold-plated hardware, and advanced vacuum tube design, the MC2000 represents the pinnacle of McIntosh's tube amplifier engineering.

Detailed Repair Information – From Our Workbench

⚡ POWER SUPPLY (Critical for MC2000)
  • Main Filter Capacitors: High-voltage electrolytic caps – we replace with 105°C rated, high-ripple current types
  • Oil-Filled Capacitors: Proprietary oil caps in power supply – we test and replace if necessary with high-quality film equivalents
  • Voltage Regulation: Complex multi-stage regulation – we verify all voltages and replace drifted components
  • B+ Voltage Verification: We verify proper plate voltage (typically 450V+) after restoration
  • Standby Circuit: Soft-start circuit components – we test and replace as needed
🎛️ COUPLING & SIGNAL CAPACITORS
CRITICAL FOR SOUND QUALITY:
  • Original coupling capacitors can drift and become leaky over time
  • We use high-quality polypropylene film capacitors (Auricap, Mundorf, or similar)
  • Output coupling caps are critical for low-frequency response and stability
  • Phase inverter coupling affects overall amplifier stability
🔋 TUBE BIAS & OUTPUT SECTION
  • Microprocessor Bias Control: The MC2000 uses a digital bias control system – we have the tools to calibrate it correctly
  • Output Tubes (KT88/6550): 8 tubes – we test all tubes on calibrated tube tester and match for balanced performance
  • Driver Tubes (12AX7, 12AT7): We test and replace weak or noisy tubes
  • Bias Adjustment Procedure: We set bias per factory specifications using the built-in meter system
  • Output Transformer Testing: We verify the Unity Coupled transformers for proper operation
🔧 RESISTORS & OTHER COMPONENTS
  • Metal Film Resistors: Most are stable, but we test all and replace drifted units
  • Screen Grid Resistors: We replace with flameproof types for safety
  • Tube Sockets: Ceramic sockets – we clean and tighten all for reliable contact
  • Controls & Switches: We clean input selector, volume control, and all switches with DeOxit
  • Remote Control Motor: We test and lubricate the motorized volume control
📊 METERS & DISPLAY
  • Blue LED Meters: We verify meter calibration and LED brightness
  • Meter Driver Circuit: We test and replace drifted components in the meter driver board
  • Brightness Control: We verify the meter brightness adjustment works correctly

Common MC2000 Problems we Solve

#1 Power Supply Capacitor Failure

Symptom: Excessive hum, low output power, overheating, intermittent operation.
Our Solution: We replace all electrolytic capacitors in the power supply. This restores proper filtering and eliminates hum.

#2 Bias System Drift

Symptom: Unstable bias readings, output tubes running hot, channel imbalance.
Our Solution: We recalibrate the microprocessor bias control system and verify all bias components. We test and match output tubes.

#3 Output Tube Failure

Symptom: Weak output, distortion, one tube glowing red, blown fuse.
Our Solution: We test all output tubes, replace weak or mismatched tubes, and provide a matched quad or octet.

#4 Leaky Coupling Capacitors

Symptom: Distortion, bias instability, poor sound quality.
Our Solution: We replace all coupling capacitors with high-quality modern film capacitors.

#5 Meter Issues

Symptom: Meters not responding, inaccurate readings, dim or flickering LEDs.
Our Solution: We test meter driver circuits, replace drifted components, and verify meter calibration.

#6 Remote Control Not Working

Symptom: Volume doesn't respond to remote, intermittent operation.
Our Solution: We test the motorized volume control, remote receiver, and control board components.

#7 Noise & Hum Issues

Symptom: Background hum, hiss, crackling.
Our Solution: We systematically identify noise sources: power supply, tube noise, resistor noise, or external interference.

#8 Standby Circuit Failure

Symptom: Amplifier won't power up, no sound after warm-up.
Our Solution: We test the soft-start circuit, standby relay, and timing components.

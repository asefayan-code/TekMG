About the Marantz Model 18 & Our Approach

The Marantz Model 18 (1967-1969) is the receiver that started it all – the first solid state receiver from Marantz and the direct predecessor to the legendary 22xx series. Produced for only two years, the Model 18 features a 40-watt-per-channel amplifier (conservatively rated), a superb FM tuner with 4 gangs, and the classic Marantz gyro-touch tuning knob and blue dial face. It's highly sought after by collectors for its rarity and exceptional sound quality. We've restored dozens of Model 18 receivers over three decades, and we treat each one with the meticulous care this American classic deserves. We use premium replacement components and follow factory service procedures to ensure your receiver performs to original specifications.

🔧 Professional Tips & Troubleshooting Techniques – From Our Workbench

💡 TIP #1: No Power / Dead Unit – Our Step-by-Step Approach
  • First, verify AC input voltage (120V) and check the power cord – we've seen many "dead" receivers that just had a bad cord.
  • Check the main fuse on the back panel – if blown, there's likely a short in the power supply or amplifier.
  • Inspect the power transformer – measure primary and secondary voltages. Open windings are rare but happen.
  • Common failure: Bridge rectifier diodes – test and replace with modern 3A/400V types.
  • Check the main filter capacitors (two large can caps) – if shorted, they'll blow the fuse.
  • Test voltage rails: +35V (main B+), +24V (tuner), -15V (bias), +12V (protection).
💡 TIP #2: Noisy / Crackling Sound – Germanium Transistor Issues
  • The Model 18 uses germanium transistors in the preamp and phono stages (early units). These are notorious for becoming noisy with age.
  • Symptoms: hiss, crackling, popping sounds, especially at low volume or with no input.
  • We test each germanium transistor with our transistor tester. Noisy ones are replaced with modern silicon equivalents (with circuit modifications).
  • Common replacements: 2SA217 → KSA992, 2SC458 → KSC1845.
  • If replacing germanium with silicon, bias adjustments are critical – we handle this carefully.
💡 TIP #3: FM Tuner Issues – Weak Reception, No Stereo, Drifting
  • The Model 18 has a superb 4-gang FM front end. If reception is weak, first check the antenna connection.
  • If stereo light is on but no stereo separation, the multiplex decoder needs alignment.
  • If the tuner drifts off station, the AFC circuit may have failed – check capacitors around the varactor diode.
  • Weak FM could be due to failed RF amplifier transistor (2SC299 or similar) – we test and replace.
💡 TIP #4: Amplifier Issues – Distortion, Low Output, One Channel Dead
  • First, check the speaker relay (if equipped) – dirty contacts cause intermittent output.
  • Test the output transistors (germanium 2N2147 or silicon replacements) – these fail frequently.
  • Check the driver transistors and bias diodes – they often fail along with outputs.
  • Replace all electrolytic capacitors on the amplifier boards – they cause distortion when aged.
  • Check and adjust bias current – factory spec is typically 20-30mA per channel.
💡 TIP #5: Hum – Power Supply Issues
  • Audible 60Hz or 120Hz hum is almost always bad power supply filter capacitors.
  • The Model 18 uses multi-section can capacitors – we rebuild or replace these with new capacitors.
  • Check ground connections – loose ground screws cause hum.
  • If hum is only in one channel, suspect bad capacitors in that channel's amplifier board.
  • After recap, verify ripple on B+ rails with an oscilloscope – should be <10mV.
💡 TIP #6: Noisy / Scratchy Controls
  • Volume, balance, tone controls, and selector switches get dirty over time.
  • We use DeoxIT D-Series for cleaning pots and Faderlube for conductive plastic controls.
  • For the volume control, we use DeoxIT Fader F5 – safe for carbon tracks.
  • If a control is still scratchy after cleaning, it may need replacement – we can source replacements.
💡 TIP #7: Dial Lamps Out / Dim – The Famous Blue Glow
  • The Model 18 uses fuse-style lamps for the dial face (typically 6.3V/200mA).
  • We replace all dial lamps with LED upgrades for brighter, cooler operation and longer life (optional).
  • If using OEM incandescent lamps, check the lamp fuse and lamp power supply.
  • The stereo beacon lamp is a separate bulb – replace if not illuminating in stereo mode.
💡 TIP #8: Phono Stage Issues – No Sound, Distortion, Hum
  • The phono preamp uses germanium transistors in early units – these are often noisy.
  • Check the phono input jacks for loose solder joints – they take mechanical abuse.
  • Replace all electrolytic capacitors in the phono stage – they cause RIAA EQ drift.
  • If hum is present, check grounding – the phono stage is very sensitive.
💡 TIP #9: Shipping Your Model 18 Safely
  • The Model 18 weighs about 40 lbs – double-boxing is essential.
  • Secure the gyro-touch tuning knob – it's fragile and unique to Marantz.
  • Remove the wood case (if present) and pack it separately to prevent damage.
  • We'll provide detailed packing instructions when you schedule service.
⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTE FROM OUR WORKBENCH: The Marantz Model 18 contains high-voltage sections (35V B+). Always discharge power supply capacitors before servicing. We use proper safety procedures. Do not attempt internal repairs unless you are a qualified technician.

Deep Dive: Our Marantz Model 18 Restoration Process

⚡ POWER SUPPLY (Critical for Reliability)
  • Main Filter Capacitors: Large multi-section can capacitors (2x 2000µF/35V typical) – we rebuild or replace with modern snap-in caps.
  • Bridge Rectifiers: We replace old selenium or silicon rectifiers with modern 3A/400V bridges.
  • Bias Supply: We rebuild the negative bias supply with fresh capacitors and diodes.
  • Regulator Section: We replace all small electrolytic capacitors on the regulator board (if equipped).
  • Safety Modifications: We can add an inrush current limiter to protect the power switch.
🎛️ PREAMPLIFIER & TONE CONTROL
COMPLETE SIGNAL PATH RESTORATION:
  • Electrolytic Capacitors: All coupling and bypass electrolytics are replaced with audio-grade types (Nichicon FG, Elna Silmic II).
  • Germanium Transistors: We test all germanium transistors (2SA217, 2SC458, etc.) – noisy ones are replaced with modern low-noise equivalents (KSC1845, KSA992) with circuit modifications.
  • Ceramic Disc Capacitors: In critical signal paths, we replace ceramic discs with polypropylene or polystyrene film capacitors.
  • Phono Stage: The phono preamp is recapped and calibrated for proper RIAA equalization.
🔊 MAIN AMPLIFIER SECTION
  • Capacitor Replacement: All electrolytic capacitors on the main amplifier board are replaced as listed in the database above.
  • Output Transistors: We test all output transistors – replace with matched modern equivalents (MJ21193/21194 or similar).
  • Driver Transistors: We test and replace driver transistors if needed.
  • Bias Adjustment: We set bias current per the service manual (typically 20-30mA).
  • DC Offset Calibration: We adjust DC offset at speaker terminals to near 0mV.
📻 FM TUNER & MULTIPLEX ALIGNMENT
  • Front End Alignment: We align the 4-gang FM front end for maximum sensitivity.
  • IF Strip Alignment: Using a sweep generator and oscilloscope, we perform a full 10.7MHz IF alignment.
  • Multiplex Decoder: We align the 38kHz oscillator and adjust separation controls for maximum stereo separation.
  • FM Detector: We calibrate the discriminator/ratio detector for lowest distortion.
  • FM Stereo Indicator: We verify proper stereo beacon operation.

Precision FM Alignment Procedure – As we Perform It

📻 OUR FM ALIGNMENT PROCESS FOR MARANTZ MODEL 18:
  • FM Front End Alignment: We align the RF amplifier, local oscillator, and mixer stages for maximum sensitivity using a precision signal generator.
  • 10.7MHz IF Alignment: Using a sweep generator and oscilloscope, we align each IF transformer for proper bandpass response and gain.
  • Discriminator / Ratio Detector: We adjust for minimum distortion and correct center tuning using a distortion analyzer.
  • Multiplex Decoder Alignment: We adjust the 38kHz oscillator and separation controls for maximum stereo separation (>35dB).
  • FM Stereo Indicator: We verify proper stereo beacon activation at the correct signal strength.
  • Result: Your Model 18 will pull in distant stations clearly, with quiet background and precise stereo imaging.

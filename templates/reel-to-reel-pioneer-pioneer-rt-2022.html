Capacitor Replacement Services for the Pioneer RT-2022

The electrolytic capacitors in the Pioneer RT-2022 are built to last, but for optimal longevity or if diagnostics show issues, targeted replacement is key. At Tek Media Group, we specialize in replacing critical capacitors, especially in the power supply, using high-quality modern equivalents (e.g., Nichicon, Panasonic) that match original values, voltages, and sizes.

Our Capacitor Replacement Process

  1. Comprehensive Testing: We evaluate all electrolytic capacitors, focusing on the power supply and oscillator board (RWR-050).
  2. Targeted Replacement: We replace critical capacitors, such as the 1000µF 30V PSU cap, if they show signs of failure (bulging, leaking, or high ESR).
  3. Optional Full Recapping: For maximum reliability, Tek Media Group offers complete capacitor replacement, guided by the service manual (available from HiFi Engine or Elektrotanya).
  4. Final Calibration: Post-replacement, we clean pots/switches with DeoxIT and calibrate your RT-2022 to factory specs for peak performance.

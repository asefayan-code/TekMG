About the McIntosh MAC 1500 & Our Service

The McIntosh MAC 1500 is a unique and highly regarded hybrid receiver from the golden age of McIntosh. Produced from 1965 to 1968, this receiver combines a tube FM tuner section with a solid-state FET preamp and a tube power amplifier – a rare hybrid design that delivers the best of both worlds: the warm, detailed sound of tubes with the reliability and low noise of early solid-state circuits. Rated at 30 watts per channel, the MAC 1500 is perfect for driving vintage speakers and delivers that classic McIntosh sound.

The McIntosh MAC 1500 – Hybrid Masterpiece

🎵 McINTOSH MAC 1500 KEY FEATURES:
  • Hybrid Design: Tube tuner + FET preamp + tube power amplifier
  • Power Output: 30 watts per channel (tube output stage)
  • FM Tuner: Sensitive FM section with automatic stereo switching
  • FET Front End: Field-effect transistors in preamp for low noise
  • Tube Complement: 4 x 6BQ5/EL84 output tubes, multiple 12AX7, 6AU6, 6BN8
  • McIntosh Output Transformers: Legendary unity-coupled output transformers
  • Brass Faceplate: Classic McIntosh aesthetic with glass front panel
  • Built-in Stereo Multiplex: FM stereo decoder with automatic switching
⚠️ IMPORTANT – MAC 1500 SPECIFIC CONSIDERATIONS: The MAC 1500 uses selenium rectifiers in the power supply that are failure-prone and can emit toxic fumes when they fail. We replace these with silicon diode assemblies. The hybrid design requires specialized knowledge of both tube and solid-state circuits. The FET preamp section uses delicate transistors that must be handled properly. We have the specific expertise and equipment to service this unique receiver.

Common McIntosh MAC 1500 Problems we Solve

#1 Electrolytic Capacitor Failure

Symptom: Excessive hum, low output power, unstable operation, noise.
Our Solution: We replace all electrolytic capacitors in the power supply, including multi-section cans.

#2 Selenium Rectifier Failure

Symptom: Low B+ voltage, burning smell, weak performance.
Our Solution: We replace with silicon diode assemblies and adjust dropping resistors for proper voltage.

#3 Weak Sensitivity / Poor FM Reception

Symptom: Only strong stations come in, weak stations are noisy or absent.
Our Solution: We perform full FM alignment, test and replace weak tubes, verify front end components.

#4 No Stereo or Poor Stereo Separation

Symptom: Stereo light doesn't come on, stereo sound is mono or distorted.
Our Solution: We align the multiplex decoder, replace weak tubes, recap the multiplex board.

#5 Distorted or Weak Sound

Symptom: Low output power, distortion, one channel weak or dead.
Our Solution: We test output tubes, bias power amplifier, replace coupling capacitors, verify output transformers.

#6 Drifted Resistors

Symptom: Off-spec voltages, poor performance, instability.
Our Solution: We test every carbon comp resistor and replace any drifted more than 10% from nominal.

#7 Weak or Dim Magic Eye Tube

Symptom: Eye tube doesn't close, dim display, no indication.
Our Solution: We test and replace weak 6E5/6U5 tubes, verify AGC circuit voltages.

#8 Noisy Controls / Intermittent Operation

Symptom: Crackling when adjusting volume or tone controls, dropouts.
Our Solution: We clean all controls and switches with DeOxit, lubricate tuning capacitor bearings.

#9 FET Preamplifier Issues

Symptom: No sound, low output, noise, distortion in preamp section.
Our Solution: We test and replace failed FETs, recap preamp board, verify power supply voltages.

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What makes the MAC 1500 unique among McIntosh receivers?

The MAC 1500 is a hybrid design combining a tube FM tuner, solid-state FET preamplifier, and tube power amplifier. This rare configuration delivers the warmth of tubes with the low noise of early solid-state circuits. Only produced from 1965-1968, it's highly sought after by collectors.

ML-3 Technical Specifications

Production Years: 1976 – 1980 (Mark Levinson Audio Systems)

Design Philosophy: Dual-monaural stereo amplifier with fully discrete circuitry, complementary symmetry output stage, and massive power supply.

Power Output

100W RMS @ 8Ω
200W RMS @ 4Ω
300W RMS @ 2Ω

Output Stage

Discrete complementary
Multiple bipolar transistors
Parallel output devices

Frequency Response

10Hz - 20kHz ±0.1dB
5Hz - 100kHz ±3dB
Slew Rate: >30V/µs

Power Supply

1.2kVA toroidal transformer
80,000µF total capacitance
±75V DC rails

Design Note: The ML-3 features completely separate left and right channel circuitry with independent power supplies, essentially two monoblock amplifiers in one chassis.

Component Failure Analysis

Based on servicing 32 ML-3 units from 1995-2023. Components listed in order of failure frequency.

Component Failure Rate Typical Issues Symptoms
Electrolytic Capacitors 100% ESR increase, capacitance loss Hum, weak bass, instability
Output Transistors 65% Thermal stress, overcurrent Distortion, blown fuses
Driver Transistors 55% Beta degradation, leakage Bias drift, offset issues
Thermal Management 50% Compound drying, joint cracks Overheating, shutdown
Power Supply Components 40% Rectifier failure, resistor drift Voltage sag, ripple increase
Common Failure Patterns:
  • Thermal cycling cracks solder joints on power devices
  • Original germanium transistors show parameter drift
  • Large filter capacitors dry out causing power supply noise
  • VAS stage transistors degrade over time affecting linearity

Testing Procedure

  1. Measure Vbe and hFE of all transistors
  2. Check for leakage at maximum rated voltage
  3. Match output devices within 5% of hFE
  4. Verify thermal tracking of bias transistors

Calibration Procedure

Bias Current

Target: 50mA per device
Total: 200mA per channel
Measure across emitter resistors

DC Offset

Target: < 10mV
Adjust: VR101, VR102
Monitor for 30 minutes

Power Supply

Rails: ±75V ±5%
Ripple: < 20mV p-p
Transformer temp: < 60°C

Setup Requirements

  • True RMS multimeter
  • 4Ω 200W dummy load
  • Oscilloscope for waveform observation
  • Thermocouple for temperature monitoring

Calibration Steps

  1. Warm up amplifier for 30 minutes
  2. Adjust bias to 50mA per output device
  3. Set DC offset to minimum (< 10mV)
  4. Monitor stability for 2 hours
  5. Perform full power test into dummy load
  6. Verify thermal stability

Common Denon AVR-A10H Problems we Solve

#1 Power Supply / Main Filter Capacitor Failure

Symptom: Excessive hum, low output power, receiver won't turn on, protection mode.
Our Solution: We replace the main filter capacitors, test rectifiers, verify all voltage rails.

#2 HDMI 2.1 Board Failure (8K Signal Issues)

Symptom: No video, intermittent signal, 8K not working, HDCP errors, eARC issues.
Our Solution: We diagnose the HDMI 2.1 board, replace failed switch ICs, repair EDID memory, restore full 8K functionality.

#3 Amplifier Channel Dropout

Symptom: One or more channels dead, distorted, or cutting out.
Our Solution: We test all 13 discrete amplifier channels, replace failed output transistors, match replacements, adjust bias.

#4 Protection Mode / Won't Power On

Symptom: Receiver goes into protection mode, clicks but no sound, won't turn on.
Our Solution: We diagnose shorted outputs, check power supply caps, verify protection circuit, repair as needed.

#5 Dirac Live Calibration Issues

Symptom: Microphone not detected, calibration fails, poor results, Dirac not working.
Our Solution: We test microphone input, verify Dirac processing module, perform full calibration, optimize filters.

#6 Speaker Relay Issues

Symptom: Relay clicks but no output, intermittent output, channel dropout.
Our Solution: We test all 13 channel relays, clean contacts, replace if faulty.

#7 Overheating / Thermal Shutdown

Symptom: Receiver shuts down when hot, protection mode after short use.
Our Solution: We clean and reapply thermal paste to all heat-sensitive components, test thermal protection circuits.

#8 No Sound / Distorted Sound

Symptom: Complete loss of sound, distorted output, HDMI audio issues.
Our Solution: We perform a full signal path diagnosis, test all audio processing ICs, check for cold solder joints, and restore proper audio output.
